# Where the Greeks Fought, 1461 to Today

An interactive map, the Greek win–draw–loss record by opponent and the full list of the 508 named land and sea battles fought by Greeks for a Greek cause,
from the fall of Trebizond (the first battle: Mangup, 1475) to the Turkish invasion of Cyprus (1974): the revolts under Venetian and Ottoman rule, the War of Independence,
the later risings, and every battle of the Greek state, at home and abroad.

**Open the page:** https://iosiflazaridis.github.io/modern-greek-battles/

- `index.html`: the page (self-contained; the data are embedded)
- `img/`: the seven images in full size (3200 × 1800); `thumb/`: their thumbnails
- `dl/`: the data as CSV, JSON and an Excel workbook (with the record tables and the 130 entries left out, with reasons)

Sources: English, Greek and Turkish Wikipedia and the standard histories, cited battle by battle in the data. Map: Natural Earth.
